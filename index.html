The GPA connection

Every check-in was matched against the GAA academic tracking roster (UCF ID first, email second). The 124 students in both datasets — the intersection cohort — let us ask: do the regulars do better in class?

Attendance vs. academic performance

Each dot = one student-semester

Each point is one student in one semester: events attended (x) against semester GPA (y). The dashed gold line is the trend across completed semesters — r = , a modest but consistently positive association. Hover any point for the student.

Read this carefully: correlation, not causation — students who choose to show up weekly may simply be the more engaged students. But the association holds within every semester independently (), which makes it worth watching.
